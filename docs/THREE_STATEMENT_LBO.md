# Integrated three-statement model and LBO: scope and audit notes

## Three statements
The forecast integrates:
- Income statement: revenue, EBITDA, depreciation, EBIT, opening-debt interest, tax and net income.
- Balance sheet: cash, receivables, net PP&E, payables, debt and retained equity.
- Cash flow statement: indirect operating cash flow, capex and debt repayments.

Each year reconciles assets to liabilities plus equity. Cash shortfalls raise an error rather than silently assuming a financing facility. The model has no dividends, new debt, acquisitions, share issuance, deferred tax, inventory or detailed working-capital ageing. Interest uses opening debt, and tax losses are not carried forward.

## LBO
The LBO module models acquisition enterprise value, seller equity value, simplified financing sources and uses, multi-tranche annual debt repayment, exit equity, gross MOIC and gross IRR. Seller debt is assumed refinanced at closing; purchase enterprise value plus transaction fees is funded with sponsor equity and new acquisition debt. No interim sponsor distributions, dividend recapitalisations, management incentives, preferred equity, fees on exit or carried interest.

## Independence
The operating three-statement forecast and LBO debt schedule are currently **separate** models. Their cash-flow assumptions are not yet integrated into a unified acquisition model; the next step is to reconcile their financing structures and incorporate a circularity-controlled interest/cash sweep.

## Illustrative only
All examples use synthetic assumptions. Valuation opinions require independent evidence for forecast drivers, multiples, financing terms and fair-value adjustments.
