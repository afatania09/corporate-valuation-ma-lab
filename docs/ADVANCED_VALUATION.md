# Preferred equity, purchase price allocation and scenario analysis

## Preferred equity waterfall
The waterfall allocates equity exit proceeds between a single **non-participating** preferred class and common equity. The preferred class receives the higher of its capped liquidation preference or its as-converted common ownership proceeds. This is not a general cap-table engine: it does not support multiple seniority tiers, participation, option pools, warrants, debt claims, dividends, ratchets, management incentive plans or carried interest. Do not apply this simplified result to real contractual instruments without legal review.

## Purchase price allocation
The PPA bridge reconciles consideration to acquired identifiable net assets and goodwill. It recognises separately identified intangible fair-value uplifts and an illustrative deferred tax liability on fair value above tax basis. It assumes 100% acquisition and does not address non-controlling interests, step acquisitions, contingent consideration, measurement-period adjustments, deferred taxes on other uplifts or asset-specific valuation techniques. A negative goodwill figure is **not** automatically a recognised bargain purchase gain: reassessment is required under applicable accounting standards.

## Scenario-weighted valuation
Scenario values are computed using EV/EBITDA less net debt with a common-equity limited-liability floor at zero, then weighted by probabilities. Probabilities must total 100%. This is not by itself an IFRS 13 compliant fair-value measurement; market-participant assumptions, valuation technique selection, calibration and other instrument rights must be analysed.

All examples are fictional, in consistent currency units, and are educational only.
